Welcome to Pigeon Forge, Tennessee

The mountain towns of Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge, Tennessee serve as popular resort destinations for solo travelers, couples, and families alike. Lying on the border of the breathtaking Great Smoky Mountains National Park, visitors have ample opportunity to get outside and explore 800 miles of hiking and biking trails, as well as miles of streams to navigate. Create memories at Ober Gatlinburg, the only ski resort in Tennessee. Hike and picnic in the Great Smoky Mountains National Park near the Little Pigeon River. Shop, dine, and experience popular attractions downtown such as Ripley’s Aquarium of the Smokies or the Titanic Museum. Wherever you go, you and yours will fall in love with Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge.
